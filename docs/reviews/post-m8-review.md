# Post-M8 quality review

Reviewed at commit 09d199d on 2026-10-01. I ran every screenshot tour, a scripted "curious kid" session in the real Electron app, a 30-minute headless soak with random grabs and flings, a grab-target audit of every starting item, and a step-time benchmark. I also read the CI history and the code behind the hints. I fixed nothing.

## Verdict

The core toy is good. Grab, fling, dizzy stars, feeding, the pond, and the day and night grading all read as Toca-quality. The plaza in particular feels alive: in five idle minutes the four plaza bugs ate, played catch, comforted each other, slipped, and posed without any help. The bug art is charming and consistent, and the menu is lovely.

The six-area world is not there yet. Away from the plaza and pond the garden is empty of life. No active bug lives in the flowerbed, the porch, or the treehouse, and the three that start in the compost lab and plaza margins are hidden until found. The porch is a dark room with 43 small props laid in a row like an inventory screen. Loose items are too small to read at 1080p. The M8 machines work, but a kid has no wordless way to learn the bench, the cauldron, or the sundial, and the payoff when the bench finishes is small.

One real bug matters most. Fast drags can launch nearby things far faster than the fling cap. Those things fly over the world's end walls and never come back. Nothing crashed, nothing went NaN, nothing fell through the ground, and saves round-tripped cleanly. I found no softlock in the four barriers.

Counts: 0 P0, 13 P1, 17 P2, 8 P3. That is 38 items.

## What I checked and found healthy

- The kid session ran 28 minutes in the real app: 14 locked flings, 12 idle minutes, two laps of all six areas with flings, feeding, pond drops, the bench, the cauldron, and time and weather changes. It logged no exceptions. The only console error was the bogus def I spawned on purpose (R33).
- The headless soak ran 30 simulated minutes with 900 random grab, drag, and fling cycles. It threw no errors and produced no NaN positions and nothing below the ground line. Its only issue was the escapes in R01. Save then load kept all 245 entities.
- I found no softlock. Rain opens the sunflower on its own. The lattice can always be pulled aside. A curled Rollo works the can tunnel if the ball is lost. The bucket lift takes items or bugs.
- In the pond, a pebble and a jar sank, a cork floated and got wet, and a paper boat stayed dry on top, all as specified.
- Bugs ate whatever was nearby, played catch, comforted each other, and recovered from dizzy spells. Food respawns, so the plaza doesn't run dry.
